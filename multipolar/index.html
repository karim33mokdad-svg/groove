A strategy game about the world as it is

MULTIPOLAR

Take a seat at a real capital. Ten live theatres. Thirty instruments of statecraft. Every action gives you a modelled forecast — with its assumptions on screen — before you commit to it.

A simulation, not a prediction. Figures are rounded open-source approximations for a 2026 baseline. Not affiliated with any government. Real conflicts kill real people — this game abstracts a human cost it cannot represent honestly.
